About

I'm a master's student in Data Mining at Shahid Beheshti University, Tehran, working under the supervision of Dr. Kheradpisheh (GPA ~18.5/20). I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science at Amirkabir University of Technology (Tehran Polytechnic), where my thesis, supervised by Dr. Shamsi, introduced ORB-SVM — a hybrid pipeline for efficient brain tumor detection from MRI scans.

My research centers on making deep learning smaller, more interpretable, and more trustworthy — particularly for medical signals and images. Much of my recent work extends Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks (KANs), replacing their spline-based edges with learnable polynomial families: a recursive second-order basis in RecKAN, and Stieltjes-Wigert q-orthogonal polynomials in SW-KAN. In parallel, I work on lightweight hybrid architectures for biomedical signal classification and co-author a survey tracing the path from S4 to Mamba across modern state space models.

Currently: preparing for the TOEFL and applying my research to phonocardiogram classification and selective state space models at Shahid Beheshti University.

Independent Researcher — RecKAN & SW-KAN

Collaborative Research, Tehran

  • Developed RecKAN, defining the KAN basis via a learnable second-order polynomial recurrence recovering Chebyshev, Fibonacci, Pell, and Jacobsthal families; outperforms parameter-matched KAN baselines on image, text, and biomedical time-series classification.
  • Developed SW-KAN, replacing B-splines with Stieltjes-Wigert q-orthogonal polynomials via a smooth exponential-of-tanh domain mapping and an O(N) three-term recurrence, outperforming 18 baseline polynomial KAN variants.
